Cinnamon's Buns Bakery

About Us

Step into our cozy bakery, where the scent of freshly baked cinnamon rolls and the sound of laughter fill the air. Cinnamon's Buns has been the heart of the Rosebank Community for over 50 years, beloved by locals and visitors alike. From our legendary buns to our delectable pastries, every bite is crafted with care and infused with the warmth of family tradition. Experience the comfort and joy that has made Cinnamon's Buns a cherished destination for generations. We invite you to join us and become a part of our sweet story.

Our Story

Cinnamon's Buns began with a simple family recipe made by great-grandmother Cinnamon. What started as a weekend treat soon became a passion which was too good not to share. From those early mornings in the home kitchen to opening the doors of our bakery, our goal has always been to spread warmth and comfort and sweetness with every swirl.

The Mission

Here at Cinnamon's Buns our mission is to create baked goods that not only satisfy your sweet tooth but also warm your heart. With the use of quality ingredients, thoughtful expressions, and a little elbow grease, and our 60-year-old Cinnamons Family Recipe, we hope that we can bring people together one treat at a time.
