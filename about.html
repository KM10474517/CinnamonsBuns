About Us




Our Story

Cinnamon’s Buns began with a simple family recipe made by great-grandmother Cinnamon.
What started as a weekend treat soon became a passion which was too good not to share.
From those early mornings in the home kitchen to opening the doors of our bakery,
our goal has always been to spread warmth and comfort and sweetness with every swirl.

Great-granny Cinnamon with her
daughter in their Home Kitchen.


The Mission

Here at Cinnamon’s Buns our mission is to create baked goods that not only satisfy
your sweet tooth but also warm your heart. With the use of quality ingredients,
thoughtful expressions, and a little elbow grease, and our 60-year-old Cinnamons Family Recipe,
we hope that we can bring people together one treat at a time.
