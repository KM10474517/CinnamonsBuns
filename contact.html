Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver?

Yes! We offer delivery within a 10km radius of our Rosebank bakery. Delivery fees start at R25. Same-day delivery available for orders placed before 2 PM.

What are your busiest times?

Our peak hours are 7-9 AM for breakfast and 12-2 PM for lunch. For quicker service, visit between 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM.

Can I pre-order for a specific time?

Absolutely! We recommend pre-ordering 24 hours in advance for specific pickup times, especially for large orders or weekends.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Currently, we don't offer gluten-free cinnamon rolls due to our traditional recipe, but we do have gluten-free cookie options. Please ask about our daily selections.
